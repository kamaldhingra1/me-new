Welcome to my AI Security Corner.

## Who am I?

I'm a cybersecurity architecture professional with 25+ years leading enterprise security, cloud security, application/product security, and — most recently — AI security transformation. My work has spanned global enterprise, financial services, hospitality, healthcare, and federal environments, which means I've seen security problems from a lot of different angles: regulated products, large-scale cloud estates, customer-facing platforms, and mission-critical government systems.

These days my focus is on securing AI — GenAI, RAG pipelines, and agentic systems — threat modeling, model/inference security, AI control planes, and secure-by-design governance for AI adoption and helping organizations build AI adoption on a foundation that's secure by design rather than secured as an afterthought.

**Credentials:** TAISE . CISSP · CISM · CCSP · CCSK · TOGAF9 · SABSA(F) · AWS AI Practitioner · Azure AI Fundamentals


## What I do
- **AI Security Architecture** — Secure AI lifecycle design, GenAI/RAG security, agentic system security, model and inference security, MLSecOps, AI threat modeling, and AI control plane design.

- **Enterprise & Cloud Security** — Zero Trust, IAM, CSPM, DevSecOps, CI/CD and software supply chain security.

- **Product & Application Security** — Secure SDLC, threat modeling, penetration testing programs, and secure-by-design governance for both regulated and non-regulated products, including medical device cybersecurity.

- **Executive & CISO Advisory** — Translating technical risk into investment roadmaps, governance models, and decisions that executives and boards can act on.
